✅ 1. Simple Grammar Correction
Use this when you just want a corrected sentence.

Prompt:
Correct the following sentence:
"He go to office yesterday."
---------------------------------------------------------------------
✅ 2. Correction with Explanation
Useful when you want to learn why it’s wrong.

Prompt:
Correct the sentence and explain the mistakes:
"He go to office yesterday."
---------------------------------------------------------------------
✅ 3. Improve Sentence (Professional Tone)
Great for resumes, emails, LinkedIn, etc.

Prompt:
Improve this sentence to sound more professional:
"I worked on many projects and did good work."
---------------------------------------------------------------------
✅ 4. Multiple Variations
Helpful when you want options.

Prompt:
Rewrite this sentence in 3 different ways:
"I handled customer issues quickly."
---------------------------------------------------------------------
✅ 5. Formal vs Informal Tone
When tone matters.

Prompt:
Rewrite this sentence in a formal tone:
"Can you send me the file asap?"
---------------------------------------------------------------------
✅ 6. Resume-Specific Correction
Very useful based on your recent work.

Prompt:
Correct and enhance this resume bullet point with better grammar and impact:
"Used Git extensively got branching, merging, and release management."
---------------------------------------------------------------------
✅ 7. Add Metrics / Impact
Perfect for job experience lines.

Prompt:
Improve this sentence by adding measurable impact:
"Improved application performance."
---------------------------------------------------------------------
✅ 8. Check for Grammar + Clarity + Conciseness
More complete refinement.

Prompt:
Check and correct the sentence for grammar, clarity, and conciseness:
"Handled, analysed and provided support for day-to-day issues in development, staging and production environments."
---------------------------------------------------------------------
✅ 9. Fix Only Grammar (No Rewrite)
If you don’t want your sentence changed too much.

Prompt:
Fix only grammar mistakes without changing the meaning:
"She don't like coffee."
---------------------------------------------------------------------
✅ 10. Advanced (Best Practice Prompt)
This gives the most useful output.

Prompt:
Correct the sentence, improve clarity, and make it professional. Also explain the changes briefly:
"Created responsive landing pages, boosting conversion rates by 15% while supporting marketing campaigns."
