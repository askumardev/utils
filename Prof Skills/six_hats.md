Scenario: Deployment Issue in a Software Development Project
Your software development team has just deployed a new feature in a client-facing application. Shortly after deployment, users begin reporting:
•Unexpected crashes on mobile devices
•Delays in data syncing across platforms
•UI inconsistencies in different browsers
The team is under pressure to fix the issues quickly, but also needs to decide whether to roll back the release, apply hotfixes, or escalate to leaders
Using Six Thinking Hats to Navigate the Situation
You gather the development team, QA, product owner, and scrum master for a structured problem-solving session using the Six Thinking Hats:

1. White Hat – Facts & Data
   •What error logs and crash reports are available?
   •How many users are affected?
   •What was the scope of the deployment and testing coverage?
2. Red Hat – Emotions & Intuition
   •How is the team feeling about the situation?
   •Is there frustration, stress, or concern about client trust?
   •What’s the gut instinct—fix or rollback?
3. Black Hat – Risks & Caution
   •What are the risks of keeping the current version live?
   •Could this affect client contracts or SLAs?
   •What’s the risk of rolling back—will it break other dependencies?
4. Yellow Hat – Optimism & Benefits
   •Can the issues be resolved quickly with targeted fixes?
   •Will this feature improve user experience once stabilized?
   •Is this an opportunity to improve deployment processes?
5. Green Hat – Creativity & Alternatives
   •Can we release a patch in stages?
   •Is there a temporary workaround for affected users?
   •Can we use feature flags to disable problematic components?
6. Blue Hat – Process & Facilitation
   •Facilitator summarizes insights from each hat.
   •Guides the team toward a decision: rollback, patch, or escalate.
   •Assigns action items and timelines for resolution.
   Why This Works in Software Projects
   •Encouragescross-functional thinkingbeyond just technical fixes.
   •Helps teams balance urgency with strategic clarity.
   •Buildscollaborative leadership and shared ownership of decisions.
   •Reduces emotional bias and reactive decision-making.
